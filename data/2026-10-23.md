  <summary>English abstract</summary>
Video foundation models have advanced rapidly in understanding and generating visual sequences, while video world models seek to capture the underlying dynamics of the world they depict. Despite their different objectives, both concern the relationship between video observations and an evolving world. This survey adopts world modeling as a unifying lens for examining these developments. Viewing an observed video as a partial, lossy, and temporally ordered projection of a latent temporal world, we organize the literature around four complementary operations: reading infers world properties from observed video; writing synthesizes video from specified or learned world conditions; sharing integrates understanding and generation through common modeling mechanisms; and interacting predicts and updates world states under actions and evolving observations. Across tasks, representations, models, datasets, and benchmarks, we trace a shift in emphasis from clip-level recognition and perceptual fidelity toward persistent state, controllable evolution, physical and causal validity, and evaluation grounded in interaction and downstream decisions, and we distill the open challenges that follow.

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) in Flying Networks (FNs) increasingly require mechanisms to translate high-level natural language objectives into executable missions. Although Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated the feasibility of language-driven UAV control, existing approaches typically rely on static model configurations and overlook mission-aware performance variability. ### [32] [Inside-JEPA: From Prediction to Relational Grounding in Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31194v1)
Inside-JEPA：从预测到潜世界模型中的关系性 grounding

*Hossein Resani,Javen Qinfeng Shi*

Main category: cs.LG

中文摘要：联合嵌入预测架构（JEPAs）在潜空间中学习世界模型，从而实现高效规划，但其状态是不透明的。我们提出 Inside-JEPA，它将面向有序实体对的关系通道置于 JEPA 潜状态内部，并表明：当逆动力学（inverse-dynamics）损失的头读取这些通道时——无论是单独读取还是作为完整状态的一部分读取——该损失无需关系标签即可对这些通道进行 grounding。在 Something-Something V2 上，仅靠潜预测的表现并不优于随机关系头，而通过从通道变化中恢复动作的逆动力学监督，在十个带有标注框的设置中将关系 AUROC 提升了 .065-.103，在任务留出的 RH20T 机器人片段上提升了 .081，并在使用现成检测与跟踪的五个关系上提升了 .050-.061。作用于实体潜变量的逆头能更准确地解码动作，但几乎不能改善关系性 grounding；打乱的标签、两个无标签目标，以及仅在 5% 的实体对上使用动作标签，同样未能对这些通道进行 grounding。给定一个动作，预测的关系变化接近观测到的未来关系的质量（AUROC .599 对 .621）。对于一步关系目标，对 16 个通道进行的有监督读出在 43% 的情况下将正确动作排在 65 个动作的前五名之内，而仅预测的潜变量为 24%，频率先验为 17%。


<details>
  <summary>English abstract</summary>
Joint-embedding predictive architectures (JEPAs) learn world models in latent space, enabling efficient planning but leaving their states opaque. We introduce Inside-JEPA, which places relational channels for ordered entity pairs inside the JEPA latent state and shows that an inverse-dynamics loss can ground these channels without relation labels when its head reads them, either alone or as part of the full state. On Something-Something V2, latent prediction alone performs no better than a random relation head, while inverse-dynamics supervision that recovers actions from channel changes improves relational AUROC by .065-.103 across ten settings with annotated boxes, by .081 on RH20T robot episodes with held-out tasks, and by .050-.061 across five relations using off-the-shelf detection and tracking. An inverse head on entity latents decodes actions more accurately but barely improves relational grounding; shuffled labels, two label-free objectives, and action labels on only 5% of the pairs also fail to ground the channels. Given an action, predicted relational changes approach the quality of observed future relations (AUROC .599 vs .621). For one-step relational goals, a supervised readout of the 16 channels ranks the correct action among the top five of 65 in 43% of cases, compared with 24% for prediction-only latents and 17% for a frequency prior.

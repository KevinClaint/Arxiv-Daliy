### [1] [Honeycomb-Surfels: Compact Hexagonal Primitives for Efficient Scene Rendering and Reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37668v1)

*Prakhar Kaushik,Soumava Paul,Alan Yuille*

Main category: cs.CV

中文摘要：新视角合成与三维重建在辐射场和高斯泼溅（splatting）的推动下发展迅速，但其内存与计算需求限制了它们在资源受限环境中的应用。我们提出 Hexels：一种新颖的平面六边形表面元素，带有一阶空间颜色场，用于高效的三维场景与二维图像表示。Hexels 利用具有平滑变化外观的局部平面区域，使用更少、更大的图元。其六边形支撑域提供了在面积与边界上都高效的域，而局部颜色场则捕捉每个图元内部的外观变化。在 34 个场景上，Hexels 在数据集上相较内存匹配的 2DGS 将 PSNR 提升 4.53–7.38 dB，同时所需训练显存更低。我们进一步通过消融实验表明，在单个图元层面，Hexels 相较 2DGS 最高提升 4.74 dB，同时使用更低的训练显存，且 FPS 快 2.31 倍。即使在无约束设置下，Hexels 也提供了互补的质量—内存权衡。在高效三维表面重建方面，Hexels 在 15 个 DTU 扫描上相较内存匹配的 2DGS 将误差降低 28.9%。在 6 个 Tanks and Temples 表面场景上，Hexels 在对比方法中取得最高的平均 F1（34.38%，而 2DGS 为 31.72%）。我们还进一步表明，Hexels 作为一种通用图元，在二维图像表示上优于 Image-GS。Hexels 在表面对齐图元的质量—效率权衡上推进了帕累托前沿。

  <summary>English abstract</summary>
Novel view synthesis and 3D reconstruction have advanced rapidly with radiance fields and splatting, yet their memory and computational demands limit their use in resource-constrained settings. We introduce Hexels: novel planar hexagonal surface elements with a first-order spatial color field for efficient 3D scene and 2D image representation. Hexels exploit locally planar regions with smoothly varying appearance using fewer, larger primitives. Their hexagonal support provides an area- and boundary-efficient domain, while the local color field captures appearance variation within each primitive. To address this, we propose XTrack, a unified framework which repurposes a pretrained video DiT with semantic and spatiotemporal priors for RGB-to-mask latent transport, jointly modeling cross-view matching and temporal tracking.   <summary>English abstract</summary>

However, language-based reasoning traces do not provide a reliable mechanism for models to revisit relevant visual evidence, leading to visual forgetting and impairing their ability to reason over video content. Existing approaches maintain visual grounding during reasoning in two main ways: they either refer to evidence using textual coordinates, which specify symbolic locations rather than the visual content itself, or reinsert cropped regions or frames into the multimodal context, preserving visual details at the cost of substantial context expansion.
